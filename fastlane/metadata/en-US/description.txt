Quick Capture turns the books you read into notes you keep.

Snap a page
• Point the camera at a printed page and snap it. The text is picked out paragraph by paragraph.
• Tap the paragraphs you want, or drag across just the lines you need, then add them to your note. Add a thought of your own as you go.
• Or tap the page and type.

Highlight and annotate
• Everything you snap lands on one page, as quoted passages.
• Select the words that matter and tap Highlight. Add a note under any passage in your own words.

Tag it
• Add hashtags such as #strategy or #leadership. Tags you've used before are suggested as you type.

Take it anywhere
• Save a Markdown note to a folder of your choice, such as your Obsidian vault, with your tags in the front matter.
• Share an A4 PDF with your highlights marked and your notes beside them.
• Send your highlights to WhatsApp, with the PDF attached if you like.
• Share to Apple Notes, Mail or Messages, or copy the page.

Private by design
• No account, no sign-up.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Your note is stored only on your iPhone.
• Text recognition runs on your device.
