Quick Capture is the fastest way to get words into a note.

There is one page, and three ways to add to it:

• Type — tap the page and start writing.
• Speak — tap Speak and talk. Your words are transcribed live and added to the page.
• Snap — point the camera at printed text, tap the passage you want, and it is added to the page.

Everything you capture lands on the same page, so a shopping list, a quote from a book and a reminder you dictated on the go all end up in one place. Edit anything by hand at any time.

When you're ready, copy the page or share it to Apple Notes, Mail, Messages or any other app.

Private by design
• No account, no sign-up.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Your note is stored only on your iPhone.
• Text recognition runs on your device, and so does speech recognition on supported iPhones.
