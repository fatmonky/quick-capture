# Privacy Policy

**Quick Capture**
*Last updated: 18 October 2026*

## Overview

Quick Capture is a note-taking app that lets you add text to a single note by typing, speaking, or snapping printed text with the camera. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data Collection

Quick Capture does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. We do not operate any servers. Your note is stored locally on your device.

## What We Don't Do

- We do not collect analytics or usage data
- We do not use third-party tracking or advertising SDKs
- We do not require an account or login
- We do not access your contacts or location
- We do not share any data with third parties

## Camera, Microphone, and Speech Recognition

Quick Capture asks for these permissions only to turn text and speech into notes:

- **Camera** — to recognise printed text you point the camera at. Text recognition runs on your device. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.
- **Microphone and Speech Recognition** — to transcribe what you say into your note. Where your device supports it, transcription runs entirely on your device. On devices that do not support on-device recognition, your audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition service under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Audio is not recorded or kept by Quick Capture.

You can turn off any of these permissions at any time in the Settings app.

## Local Data

The only data Quick Capture stores is the text of your note. It lives on your device and may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, as with other app data. It is never sent to us.

When you choose to share or copy your note (for example, to Apple Notes), it goes only where you send it.

If you delete the app, all data is permanently removed from your device.

## Children's Privacy

Quick Capture does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app does not collect information from any user.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/fatmonky/quick-capture).
