# Privacy Policy

**Quick Capture**
*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

## Overview

Quick Capture is a note-taking app that lets you snap printed text with the camera, or type, into a single note that you can highlight, annotate and tag. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data Collection

Quick Capture does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. We do not operate any servers. Your note is stored locally on your device.

## What We Don't Do

- We do not collect analytics or usage data
- We do not use third-party tracking or advertising SDKs
- We do not require an account or login
- We do not access your contacts or location
- We do not share any data with third parties

## Camera

Quick Capture asks for camera access only to turn printed text into notes. Text recognition runs on your device. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.

You can turn off camera access at any time in the Settings app.

## Local Data

The only data Quick Capture stores is the text of your note, its tags, the tags you have used before, and the folder you chose for Markdown notes. It lives on your device and may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, as with other app data. It is never sent to us.

When you choose to save, share or copy your note (for example, to a Markdown folder, Apple Notes, a PDF or WhatsApp), it goes only where you send it.

If you delete the app, all data is permanently removed from your device.

## Children's Privacy

Quick Capture does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app does not collect information from any user.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/fatmonky/quick-capture).
